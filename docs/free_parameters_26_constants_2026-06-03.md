## The intel

The observed universe, at the level of the Standard Model of particle physics
plus minimal cosmology, is fully specified by a small finite set of
**dimensionless free parameters that are measured, not derived**. The canonical
count is **26 fundamental constants**.

This is established physics, not a nn_universe result. It matters here only as
the *target* any "universe-as-deep-layered-sequential-computation" framing
(Appendix D) would ultimately have to reproduce.

## The standard accounting (26)

**Standard Model — 19:**

| Group | Count | Parameters |
|---|---|---|
| Charged-fermion masses | 9 | 6 quark masses + 3 charged-lepton masses (e, μ, τ) |
| Quark mixing (CKM) | 4 | 3 mixing angles + 1 CP-violating phase |
| Gauge couplings | 3 | $g_1$ (U(1)$_Y$), $g_2$ (SU(2)$_L$), $g_3$ (SU(3)$_c$) |
| Higgs sector | 2 | vacuum expectation value $v$ + Higgs mass / quartic $\lambda$ |
| Strong CP | 1 | QCD vacuum angle $\theta_{\mathrm{QCD}}$ |

**Neutrino + lepton-mixing extension — 7:**

| Group | Count | Parameters |
|---|---|---|
| Neutrino masses | 3 | $m_1, m_2, m_3$ (Dirac treatment) |
| Lepton mixing (PMNS) | 4 | 3 mixing angles + 1 CP-violating phase |

**Total: 19 + 7 = 26.**

## Why the count is sometimes quoted differently (25–31)

The figure is convention-dependent. Honest hedging requires flagging this:

- **Majorana neutrinos** add 2 Majorana CP phases → 28.
- **Dark energy / cosmological constant** $\Lambda$ (or dark-energy density
  $\Omega_\Lambda$) is often added when "cosmology" is included → +1. This is the
  *hardest* entry — even mainstream theory cannot derive it, and it is radiatively
  unstable. See `docs/cosmological_constant_missing_law_2026-06-03.md`.
- **$\theta_{\mathrm{QCD}}$** is sometimes dropped (it is measured consistent
  with zero) → 25.
- Newton's $G$ / the Planck scale is sometimes counted as a dimensional input,
  but as a *dimensionless* count it is absorbed into the choice of units.

We adopt **26** as the canonical figure (19 SM + 3 neutrino masses + 4 PMNS),
matching the most common "Standard Model + Dirac neutrinos" statement.

## Structural relevance to the substrate hypothesis (conjectured, open)

*Conjectured.* If the universe's fundamental substrate is a deep-layered-
sequential computation (Appendix D; Vanchurin 2020), then the 26 constants are
the analogue of a network's **irreducible parameterisation** — closer to
non-learnable architectural hyperparameters than to trained weights. The
substrate hypothesis would, in its strong form, have to *derive* (or at least
constrain) these 26 numbers rather than take them as input.

This is the hard form of the open problem. nn_universe makes **no claim** to do
this. The FIM tier hierarchy result is a *necessary-signature* statement about
the substrate's computational class (deep-layered-sequential vs spatially-
parallel QFT), not a generative theory of the constants. Recording the count
here only fixes the quantitative bar any such generative claim must clear.

## What is established vs. conjectured

- **Established:** the universe has ~26 measured fundamental constants; the
  count is convention-dependent in the 25–31 range.
- **Conjectured / open:** that these map onto an irreducible substrate
  parameterisation; that a deep-layered-sequential substrate could derive them;
  that the FIM tier hierarchy bears on the count at all.
